About

Hi, I’m Stella Ma, an undergraduate at Cornell University studying Statistics and Biometry (expected graduation May 2028). I work as a research assistant at several Cornell labs and centers, on survey research, benchmark design, and reproducibility in the social sciences. Outside of research, I enjoy hiking, playing guitar, and drawing comics.

Research interests

I’m a statistics student interested in how we gather, evaluate, and draw conclusions from data about people. My work spans:

  • Representativeness and survey research: how sampling design, weighting, and nonresponse shape what a dataset can tell us about a population.
  • Evaluation methods: statistical approaches to benchmarking and auditing, including LLM performance on policy tasks and the reproducibility of published research.
  • Statistics for the social sciences: applying causal inference and econometric methods to questions in education, labor, and public policy.
